SECTION 1: SCHEDULING CHANNELS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Patients may schedule appointments through the following channels:

1.1 Online Patient Portal
   URL: mypatientportal.healthorg.com
   Available: 24 hours/day, 7 days/week
   Features: Book, modify, cancel appointments; view provider availability; receive instant confirmation.

1.2 Mobile App
   App: MyHealthConnect™ (iOS and Android)
   Available: 24 hours/day, 7 days/week
   Features: Same as patient portal plus push notifications.

1.3 Phone Scheduling
   Number: (555) 123-4567
   Hours: Monday – Friday: 7:00 AM – 7:00 PM | Saturday: 8:00 AM – 2:00 PM
   For after-hours urgent (non-emergency) needs, our nurse triage line is available at (555) 123-4599.

1.4 In-Person
   At any of our clinic registration desks during operational hours.

SECTION 2: APPOINTMENT TYPES AND LEAD TIMES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1 Same-Day Appointments
Available for acute minor illnesses (cold, flu-like symptoms, UTI, minor rash, etc.) on a first-come, first-served basis. Same-day slots open at 7:00 AM. Call or book online early, as slots fill quickly.

2.2 Routine / Wellness Appointments
Typically available within 3–7 business days. Includes annual physicals, wellness exams, chronic disease management, and preventive screenings.

2.3 Specialist Consultations
Typically available within 7–21 business days depending on specialty and urgency. A referral from your primary care provider may be required by your insurance plan.

2.4 Urgent Care Appointments
For conditions that need prompt attention but are not life-threatening (suspected fractures, lacerations requiring stitches, high fever, moderate infections). Available Monday–Friday until 5:00 PM; Saturday until 1:00 PM.

2.5 Procedure Appointments
Colonoscopy, minor surgical procedures, infusions, and imaging studies require pre-scheduling with adequate preparation time. Lead times vary: 1–6 weeks depending on the procedure.

2.6 Annual Physical and Preventive Screenings
We recommend scheduling your annual physical and preventive screenings (mammogram, colonoscopy, bone density) at least 4–6 weeks in advance. These can be combined with your primary care visit.

SECTION 3: CANCELLATION AND NO-SHOW POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

3.1 Cancellation Policy
We ask that you cancel or reschedule appointments at least 24 hours in advance. This allows us to offer that time slot to another patient in need.

To cancel: Log in to the patient portal, use the mobile app, or call (555) 123-4567.

3.2 Late Cancellation (less than 24 hours)
Late cancellations (within 24 hours of the appointment) may incur a $35 administrative fee. This fee is not covered by insurance.

3.3 No-Show Policy
A no-show is defined as failing to attend a scheduled appointment without prior notice. No-show fees:
   • First no-show:   $35 administrative fee.
   • Second no-show:  $50 administrative fee and a mandatory phone check-in before next scheduling.
   • Third no-show:   $75 fee and potential transfer to walk-in-only scheduling status pending review.

3.4 Exceptions
Fees are waived for: documented medical emergencies, transportation failures with advance notification, severe weather (at clinic management's discretion), and first-time patients.

SECTION 4: CHECK-IN REQUIREMENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4.1 Arrival Time
Please arrive 15 minutes early for your first appointment with a new provider and 10 minutes early for all subsequent visits to complete check-in.

4.2 Required Documents at Check-In
   • Valid government-issued photo ID (driver's licence, passport, state ID).
   • Current insurance card(s) — both primary and secondary if applicable.
   • Completed registration/intake forms (if not submitted online).
   • Referral documentation (if required by your insurance plan).
   • List of current medications, supplements, and allergies.
   • Co-payment (credit/debit card, HSA/FSA card, or cash).

4.3 Late Arrivals
Patients arriving more than 10 minutes late for a routine appointment may be asked to reschedule. Patients arriving up to 20 minutes late for a 30+ minute appointment will be accommodated when provider schedule allows.

SECTION 5: SPECIAL SCHEDULING CONSIDERATIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

5.1 Language Interpretation Services
We offer professional medical interpretation services in over 150 languages at no cost. Request an interpreter when scheduling your appointment. Same-day requests are accommodated based on availability.

5.2 Accessibility Accommodations
Patients requiring wheelchair access, sign language interpretation, large-print materials, or other accommodations should notify us when scheduling. All our facilities are ADA-compliant.

5.3 Paediatric Appointments
Patients under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian at all appointments, except for services where state law permits minor consent (e.g., sexual health, substance use, mental health in certain states).

5.4 Chaperone Policy
Patients may request a chaperone (clinic staff member) for any examination. Providers may also request a chaperone present. This is documented in the visit record.

SECTION 6: APPOINTMENT REMINDERS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

You will receive appointment reminders via:
   • Email: 72 hours before the appointment.
   • Text message (SMS): 48 hours before.
   • Phone call: 24 hours before (for patients who opted in).

To update your communication preferences, log in to the patient portal → Profile → Notification Settings.
