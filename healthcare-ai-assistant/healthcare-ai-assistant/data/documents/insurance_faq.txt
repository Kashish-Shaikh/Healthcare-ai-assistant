SECTION 2: CO-PAYMENTS, DEDUCTIBLES, AND OUT-OF-POCKET COSTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q: What is a co-payment (co-pay)?
A: A co-payment is a fixed amount you pay for a covered service, regardless of the total bill. For example, if your plan has a $30 primary care co-pay, you pay $30 whether the visit costs $150 or $250. Co-pays are due at the time of service.

Q: What is a deductible?
A: A deductible is the amount you must pay out-of-pocket before your insurance begins sharing costs. For example, if your deductible is $1,500 and you've paid $800 toward it this year, you still owe the next $700 before insurance coverage kicks in. Many preventive services are exempt from deductibles even on high-deductible plans.

Q: What is coinsurance?
A: After meeting your deductible, coinsurance is your share of covered medical costs. If your coinsurance is 20%, and the allowed charge is $200, you pay $40 and your insurance pays $160.

Q: What is an out-of-pocket maximum?
A: This is the most you'll have to pay for covered services in a plan year. Once you reach your out-of-pocket maximum, your insurance covers 100% of covered services for the rest of the year.

Q: When is payment due?
A: Co-payments are collected at check-in (in-person) or before the telehealth visit begins. Any balance not covered by insurance is billed within 30 days of claim adjudication.

SECTION 3: INSURANCE VERIFICATION AND PRIOR AUTHORISATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q: How do you verify my insurance?
A: We verify insurance eligibility electronically before every appointment. To ensure accurate verification:
   • Bring your current insurance card (front and back) to every visit.
   • Notify us immediately of any insurance changes.
   • For new patients, provide insurance details when scheduling.

Q: What is prior authorisation?
A: Prior authorisation (PA) is approval from your insurance company required before certain services (specialist visits, imaging, surgery, certain medications). Without PA, your insurance may deny coverage. Our staff initiates the PA process — this can take 3–10 business days.

Q: What happens if my prior authorisation is denied?
A: You have the right to appeal. Our team will:
   (1) Notify you of the denial within 2 business days.
   (2) Provide written rationale from the insurer.
   (3) Assist with the appeals process, including peer-to-peer reviews.
   You may also contact your state's Insurance Commissioner for assistance.

SECTION 4: BILLING AND STATEMENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q: When will I receive my bill?
A: After your insurance processes the claim (typically 30–45 days post-visit), you will receive a statement for any remaining balance via mail and/or through the patient portal.

Q: What is an Explanation of Benefits (EOB)?
A: An EOB is a document from your insurance company explaining what they paid, what was adjusted, and what you owe. It is NOT a bill. Compare your EOB with our bill to ensure accuracy.

Q: Can I pay my bill online?
A: Yes. Pay online at patientportal.healthorg.com/billing using credit card, debit card, HSA/FSA card, or bank transfer. Payment plans are available for balances over $200.

Q: What payment plan options are available?
A: We offer interest-free payment plans:
   • Balances $200–$999:    Up to 6 monthly payments.
   • Balances $1,000–$4,999: Up to 12 monthly payments.
   • Balances $5,000+:       Up to 24 monthly payments (requires application).
   Contact Patient Financial Services at (555) 456-7890 to enrol.

Q: What if I cannot afford my bill?
A: We offer:
   • Charity Care: For patients at or below 200% of the Federal Poverty Level, eligible charges may be forgiven. Apply at any registration desk or download the form from our website.
   • Sliding Scale Fees: Income-based reduced rates.
   • Payment Plans: As described above.
   • Financial Counselling: Free one-on-one sessions with our Patient Financial Advocates.
   No patient will be denied emergency care based on inability to pay.

SECTION 5: MEDICARE AND MEDICAID
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q: Do you accept Medicare?
A: Yes. We are enrolled Medicare providers. We accept Original Medicare (Parts A and B) and most Medicare Advantage plans. Annual Wellness Visits and most preventive screenings are covered at 100% with no co-pay under Medicare.

Q: Are there services Medicare doesn't cover?
A: Medicare does not cover routine dental, vision (except after cataract surgery), hearing aids, or most cosmetic procedures. We can provide a cost estimate before scheduling these services.

Q: Do you accept Medicaid?
A: Yes. We accept Medicaid for most services. Coverage varies by state and managed care organisation. Please present your Medicaid card at check-in.

Q: How do I dispute a charge?
A: To dispute a charge:
   (1) Call Patient Financial Services at (555) 456-7890.
   (2) Submit a written dispute via the patient portal.
   Disputes are investigated within 30 days. Billing is suspended during investigation.

Q: How long are my medical and billing records kept?
A: Medical records are maintained for a minimum of 10 years for adults (or until the patient's 21st birthday for minors). Billing records are kept for 7 years in compliance with tax regulations.
